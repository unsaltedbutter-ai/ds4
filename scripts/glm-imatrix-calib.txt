===== DS4_IMATRIX_PROMPT 2 =====
[gMASK]<sop><|user|>
Write a Python function that returns the length of the longest strictly increasing run in a list of integers.<|assistant|>
Here is a linear-time solution:

def longest_increasing_run(nums):
    if not nums:
        return 0
    best = current = 1
    for i in range(1, len(nums)):
        if nums[i] > nums[i - 1]:
            current += 1
            best = max(best, current)
        else:
            current = 1
    return best

It scans the list once, tracking the current run length and the best seen so far, using constant extra space.

===== DS4_IMATRIX_PROMPT 6 =====
[gMASK]<sop><|user|>
Explain how a mitochondrion produces energy, in plain language.<|assistant|>
A mitochondrion breaks down nutrients and uses the released energy to pump protons across its inner membrane, creating a gradient. As those protons flow back through an enzyme called ATP synthase, the enzyme spins and stitches together ATP, the molecule cells spend as energy. Mitochondria were once free-living bacteria, which is why they keep their own small genome.

===== DS4_IMATRIX_PROMPT 9 =====
[gMASK]<sop><|user|>
What is the difference between weather and climate?<|assistant|>
Weather is the state of the atmosphere over hours or days - the rain this afternoon or tonight's cold. Climate is the long-run statistical summary of weather over decades. A warming climate can still include an unusually cold winter, because climate describes the distribution from which any single day's weather is drawn.

===== DS4_IMATRIX_PROMPT 13 =====
[gMASK]<sop><|user|>
Why does a rainbow always appear at a fixed angle from the point opposite the Sun?<|assistant|>
Raindrops refract and reflect sunlight, and the geometry of that bending sends light back most strongly at about 42 degrees from the antisolar point - the spot directly opposite the Sun. Because the angle is fixed by the physics of refraction in water, the rainbow always forms a circle at that angular radius, arching higher when the Sun is low and flattening when the Sun is high.

Give three concise tips for writing a clear technical explanation.<|assistant|>

===== DS4_IMATRIX_PROMPT 16 =====
[gMASK]<sop><|user|>
Briefly: why did writing first develop for accounting rather than literature?<|assistant|>
The earliest scripts recorded debts and inventories - grain, livestock, and labor owed - because trade and administration needed durable, transferable records that memory could not reliably hold. Only once marks could fix quantities did writing later stretch to capture law, story, and argument.
